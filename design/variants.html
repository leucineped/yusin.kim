What 43 installed skills actually cost

August 1, 2026 · 1,156 words

Budding — the shape is right, details may move.

I assumed the skills I never use were quietly eating my context window. I measured it. All 43 of them cost about 1,600 tokens. The one skill I actually use costs 22,000 in a single call.

The always-on cost is the listing: names plus descriptions, injected into every session. The body of a skill loads only when it is invoked. Those are different numbers, and people conflate them.
